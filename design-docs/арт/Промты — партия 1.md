# Промты — партия 1

Каждый промт самодостаточен: сцена + четыре обязательных блока. Копировать целиком.

---

## Новые обязательные блоки

### Блок A — плотность детали (правка «грязновато»)

> Render everything in LARGE SIMPLE FLAT SHAPES — broad clean planes with only a few decisive folds; no hatching, no cross-hatching, no scribbled line texture, no stippling, no dense small scratchy marks, no clutter of tiny trophy, no rust speckle, no grime spatter. Wear and dirt are shown as a few large flat colour patches, never as fine marks. Surfaces stay smooth and uncluttered; the image must read at thumbnail size.

### Блок B — цветоразделение (правка «серо и монохромно»)

> Colour-separate the gear and hardware: every figure and machine carries two or three clearly different hues — body or fabric in one colour, armour plates and panels in a distinctly different one, straps, boots and fittings in a third — all held at muted, restrained saturation so nothing competes with the single bright accent. No monochrome figures, no single-hue grey mass: parts must be told apart at a glance from a distance, while the overall effect stays sober and military, never bright, candy-coloured or toylike.

### Блок C — стилевой блок (§7.2, дословно)

> cel-shaded comic illustration, semi-realistic adult proportions, flat colour fills with two-to-three tone hard-edged shading, crisp clean shadow shapes; COLOURED linework — the outline colour is a darkened cooler version of each local colour (deep indigo on blue fabric, warm sienna on skin, blue-grey on white panels, dark emerald on the crystal), absolutely no pure black lines; variable line weight, heavier on the outer silhouette, thinner inside forms, and the line thins out and breaks where direct light falls on the form; interior detail described by shadow shapes rather than outlines; one cool dominant ambient light plus one small bright practical light source in frame casting warm up-light on hands and faces; crisp cool rim light along shoulders, jaw, hair and edges; muted military palette with a single saturated accent colour on about 8% of the frame; depth conveyed by lowering contrast toward the background, never by blur; perfectly clean surfaces — no brush texture, no grain, no airbrush gradients

### Блок D — негатив (расширенный)

> Negative: hatching, cross-hatching, scribbled linework, stippling, ink texture, sketchy scratchy strokes, busy micro-detail, visual clutter, trophy scatter, grime spatter, rust speckle, monochrome figures, single-hue grey mass, WWI trench warfare, WWII period gear, steampunk, dieselpunk, pure black outlines, heavy black ink contour, uniform thick outline on everything, airbrush, soft gradients, painterly brushstrokes, oil painting, 3D render, glossy plastic, subsurface scattering, ray tracing, anime, manga, chibi, big eyes, children's cartoon, caricature, neon cyberpunk magenta and purple, lens flare, bloom, depth of field, motion blur, film grain, paper texture, noise, readable text, letters, logos, watermark, signature

## 1. Склад трофеев — гигантские чёрные шестерни

*Сюжет: склад Механиков, где трофеи хранятся как валюта — исполинские тёмные шестерни в стеллажах. Рабочие ставят одну на ребро крюком. Свет — янтарная рабочая лампа на кран-балке.*

> Interior of a Mechanics salvage depot, medium-wide shot from a low angle, tight framing. Rows of steel racks and a heavy overhead gantry hold GIANT machined gear wheels — oversized graphite-dark cog discs, waist-high and taller, stacked leaning against one another and slotted upright in the racks like bullion in a vault; they are the depot's salvage tokens and read as deliberately icon-like: bold simple silhouettes, blunt teeth, a clean round hub, no fussy machinery detail. Two workers heave one gear upright with a hook bar — olive-khaki coveralls, slate-blue composite chest plates, oxblood leather aprons, brass-toned buckles; a third stands checking a stack, sleeves rolled, forearms tense, a sealed near-future respirator pushed up on his forehead. Welded seams, rivets, stencilled numerals and orange hazard striping on the racks and floor. Cold blue-grey ambient light from high dusty roof windows sets the palette; a single caged amber work lamp clamped to the gantry is the only practical light source in frame, throwing warm up-light onto their hands, jaws and the rim of the nearest gear and striking one hard flat highlight across the dark metal. Slate, olive and concrete palette with one saturated amber accent. The gears are deep graphite grey, never pure black.

## 2. Склад контейнеров — три штуки, открытые и закрытые

*Сюжет: склад снабжения, ровно три сброшенных контейнера: закрытый с парашютом на крышке, вскрытый с кристаллом внутри (он и светит), опустошённый. Интендант пересчитывает.*

> Interior of a supply depot under a corrugated composite roof, low three-quarter shot, tight framing. Exactly three airdropped military containers, nothing more: one sealed and intact, crumpled parachute silk still bundled on its lid, corners scarred from impact; one cracked open with the lid tilted back, packed full of jagged luminous green crystal shards; one wide open and half-emptied, showing plain moulded cases and a coiled cable inside. A quartermaster leans over the open crate counting on his fingers — khaki coverall, slate-blue plate carrier, oxblood gloves and boots; behind him a younger soldier in a sealed near-future respirator and olive undersuit drags a case clear of the stack. Bare swept concrete floor, a couple of large plain sandbag stacks, deliberately uncluttered space. Cold flat blue-grey ambient light; the crystal inside the cracked container is the single practical light source, throwing toxic green up-light onto the quartermaster's face and hands and onto the underside of the tilted lid. Khaki, slate and concrete palette with one saturated poison-green accent.

## 3. Золотые монеты на столе командного центра

*Сюжет: горсть тяжёлых золотых монет с крупными вдавленными цифрами 1 и 5 на столе-карте в ЦУ, рука офицера двигает столбик монет. Монетные цифры — единственные читаемые знаки в кадре.*

> Close-up, low three-quarter shot across a command-centre map table. A loose spill of thick gold coins lies on the steel tabletop beside a folded field map — some stacked, some flat, some on edge; heavy blank discs bearing only a single large embossed numeral on the face, the digit 1 on the smaller coins and the digit 5 on the larger ones, and no other text, letters or markings anywhere in the frame. A gloved officer's hand pushes a short stack of them across the table; at the frame edge a second figure's forearm is cropped, cuff and slate-blue plate almost in silhouette. Behind them the modular interior of the Control Centre: plain panels, an antenna cluster, a radar dish strut, screens carrying only dim abstract glyph noise. Cold blue interior ambient light sets the palette; a small amber table lamp set low beside the coins is the single practical light source, throwing warm up-light onto the coins, the fingers and the underside of the officer's cuff, with one hard flat highlight on each coin's rim. Blue-grey palette with olive and oxblood on the uniforms and one saturated warm gold accent; the gold is the brightest thing in frame.

## 4. Солдат лезет на вышку с огромной турелью

*Сюжет: экстремальный ракурс снизу — солдат на лестнице вышки, ближняя нога подошвой почти в камеру, тело резко уходит вверх, над ним нависает исполинская турель. Светит кристалл у основания вышки.*

> Extreme low angle looking almost straight up, strong perspective and heavy foreshortening, tight framing. A soldier climbs the ladder of a dug-in defensive tower: one boot is thrust toward the camera and dominates the lower frame, its sole and heavy tread enormous in foreshortening, while the rest of his body recedes sharply upward — khaki coverall, slate-blue composite shoulder plates, oxblood harness straps and belt, sealed near-future respirator with a slim integrated filter, one hand gripping the rung above his head. The tower is built from large salvaged plates, taut tarpaulin and heavy timber, bolted together and banked with sandbags at its base. Crowning it, an ENORMOUS turret looms against the sky, seen from directly beneath: a massive blunt gun mount with a thick barrel and armoured cheeks, its silhouette flattening into low-contrast planes as it recedes. Low grey dust-choked sky above. Cold flat ambient light from the overcast sky; a cluster of jagged luminous green crystal breaking through the cracked earth at the tower's footing is the single practical light source, throwing toxic green up-light onto the sole of his boot, the ladder rungs and the underside of the sandbags. Ochre, khaki and slate palette with one saturated poison-green accent.
