ПРОМТ — фон задника коробки: полевая база у грядки (v1, 21.10.2026)

Для чего: верхняя сцена задника коробки (tools/книга/задник_коробки.py, полоса
3200×1430 точек, то есть около 2,2 : 1). Генерировать в 16:9 по ширине
наибольшим размером, потом подрезать сверху и снизу. Слева треть кадра пустая
и тёмная — под логотип и текст; низ пустой — у нижнего края стоят лидеры
наций (их картинки накладываются отдельно, поэтому крупных людей в сцене нет).

Собран по стиль-библии (design-docs/арт/Иллюстрации — стиль-библия и генератор
промтов.md): формула §7.1, блоки §4.5–4.8, §5.4, §7.2 дословно, негатив §7.3
плюс негатив анатомии; без собственных имён мира (запрет 2). По запрету 3
(сцена, не юнит на белом) в дословных блоках заменены «military» и «armour»:
workwear, appliqué plates, utilitarian.

Положить готовую картинку в «Иллюстрации/фоны и сцены» на Диске и поменять
номер фона в задник_коробки.py (АРТ = ...[75]) или указать путь явно.

=== PROMPT ===
Wide panoramic high three-quarter view across a vast ochre and khaki scorched wasteland under a low overcast grey dusty sky. Right of centre, a broad patch of cracked earth torn open by jagged faceted luminous green crystal growths, the brightest spot in the frame, throwing poison-green up-light onto everything near it. Around the crystal patch, at a clear distance from it, a deployed forward base built from airdropped modules: a large modular composite command station with a big satellite dish and antenna masts, a squat plated extraction plant dug in beside the crystal patch with thick cables running to it, a compact power station with warm amber glowing cells, and a tall static tower on a broad sandbagged base. In the midground a column of heavy tracked vehicles in olive-grey with slate-blue appliqué plates rolls through the dust toward the crystal; three angular aircraft with canted twin tails cross the sky above; several dented supply crates descend on drab parachutes, one already landed with its canopy crumpled on the ground. On the far horizon, smooth seamless pale grey-white ruins of unknown architecture sit half-sunk in the ground. Tiny distant figures in technical coveralls work near the vehicles, small for scale. Composition: the whole left third of the frame is a calm, dark, low-detail area of dusty sky and empty ground with no objects; the lower quarter is calm open ground with no objects; the base, crystal and vehicles fill the right two thirds and run off the right and top edges. Cold flat ambient light, the crystal patch as the single bright practical source, warm amber work lights on the base, crisp cool rim light along vehicle edges and structures. Ochre and khaki palette with a saturated poison-green accent on about 8% of the frame. NEAR-FUTURE SCI-FI ALTERNATE HISTORY, never a historical period: uniforms are sharply tailored modern-futurist workwear — smooth technical fabrics, clean seam lines, sealed zip plackets, standing mandarin collars, magnetic closures instead of button rows, slim shoulder yokes with integrated hard composite epaulettes and stiff forearm cuffs, rank shown as small machined metal tabs; equipment is matte moulded composite with flush recessed panels and thin light strips. Silhouettes are crisp, disciplined and expensive-looking, cut close to the body. No period costume — no WWI or WWII uniforms, no cloth peaked service caps, no rows of brass buttons, no wool greatcoats, no shirt-and-tie, no medal clusters, no chrome, no neon cyberpunk panels. ANATOMY MUST BE CORRECT for every person in the frame: each has exactly one head, one neck, two arms and two legs; each arm ends in one hand with exactly five clearly separated fingers — four fingers and one thumb — and each leg ends in one foot; shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees and ankles are all present and correctly jointed; limb lengths are natural for an adult body and each hand is sized correctly for its wrist. Every visible arm can be traced back to the shoulder of its own body, and every object is held by one identifiable hand belonging to one identifiable person. Each hand is either fully visible and complete or clearly outside the frame, never half-drawn. Poses are simple, open and unambiguous, with clear space between figures so no two bodies overlap and no limb crosses another person. Negative: extra limbs, third arm, third leg, duplicated hands, missing hand, missing arm, floating disembodied hand, fused fingers, six fingers, four fingers, malformed hands, mitten hands, claw hands, deformed faces, twisted joints, backwards knees, merged overlapping bodies. PHYSICAL LOGIC MUST HOLD. Every object occupies its own separate volume: nothing passes through anything else, nothing is embedded in anything else, and no part of one object emerges from the surface of another. Items stand well apart with clear open floor between them, each resting stably on its own stand, trestle, cradle or the floor, with visible supports. People stand or kneel in places where a body physically fits, on the outside of the objects they work on, never inside or underneath them, and no figure blocks or occludes another figure who is working. Surfaces are solid and closed: every opening exists for a reason and is explained by its own removed cover or hatch lying nearby, and there are no random square holes, no unexplained cut-outs, no perforated panels. Render everything in LARGE SIMPLE FLAT SHAPES — broad clean planes with only a few decisive folds; no hatching, no cross-hatching, no scribbled line texture, no stippling, no dense small scratchy marks, no clutter of tiny trophy, no rust speckle, no grime spatter. Wear and dirt are shown as a few large flat colour patches, never as fine marks. Surfaces stay smooth and uncluttered; the image must read at thumbnail size. Colour-separate the gear and hardware: every figure and machine carries two or three clearly different hues — body or fabric in one colour, appliqué plates and panels in a distinctly different one, straps, boots and fittings in a third — all held at muted, restrained saturation so nothing competes with the single bright accent. No monochrome figures, no single-hue grey mass: parts must be told apart at a glance from a distance, while the overall effect stays sober and utilitarian, never bright, candy-coloured or toylike. cel-shaded comic illustration, semi-realistic adult proportions, flat colour fills with two-to-three tone hard-edged shading, crisp clean shadow shapes; COLOURED linework — the outline colour is a darkened cooler version of each local colour (deep indigo on blue fabric, warm sienna on skin, blue-grey on white panels, dark emerald on the crystal), absolutely no pure black lines; variable line weight, heavier on the outer silhouette, thinner inside forms, and the line thins out and breaks where direct light falls on the form; interior detail described by shadow shapes rather than outlines; one cool dominant ambient light plus one small bright practical light source in frame casting warm up-light on hands and faces; crisp cool rim light along shoulders, jaw, hair and edges; muted utilitarian palette with a single saturated accent colour on about 8% of the frame; depth conveyed by lowering contrast toward the background, never by blur; perfectly clean surfaces — no brush texture, no grain, no airbrush gradients
