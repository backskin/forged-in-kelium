# ФОНЫ — МИРНЫЕ ВАРИАНТЫ (13 промтов, без боя)

**Что это.** Те же тринадцать сюжетов, что и в боевом файле, но **без единой воронки, без гари и без разрушений**. Место выглядит старым и брошенным, а не обстрелянным: выцветшие поверхности, песчаные наносы, пыль, трещины, запертые пустые здания.

**Дорог заметно меньше.** Где была магистраль — теперь либо узкая дорога, срезающая угол, либо грунтовка, либо дороги нет вообще (поля 2, 5, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12, 13).

**Тональное окно строгое, без исключения на гарь:** самый тёмный тон — средний серый, ничто не приближается к чёрному. Техники, обломков и металлолома нет вообще.

Low in saturation, high in value: soft, never vivid, never dark, no saturated green.

CONTENTS OF THIS FIELD, a counted list — draw these and nothing more: ONE huge concrete apron filling almost the whole frame — a paved open square of large cast slabs with straight expansion joints running in two directions, a few faded painted markings and bay outlines, THREE shallow service pits and drainage channels as clean flat recesses, and a low kerb along ONE side only; sand drifted over it from ONE corner in broad tongue-shaped shapes with clean crisp outlines, thinning to bare slab across the middle; a few faint drag scars. THERE ARE NO BUILDINGS, NO ROADS, NO WALLS, NO FENCES, NO POLES AND NO STRUCTURES OF ANY KIND on this apron.

SCALE IS SET BY COMPARING OBJECTS WITH EACH OTHER: one cast slab is a few paces across, a service pit is several slabs long, and the sand tongues run over dozens of slabs at once.

THE SURFACE ITSELF IS THE SUBJECT AND IT IS BUILT, NOT BLANK: the apron reads as LARGE flat shapes — big cast slabs of very slightly different tone, straight joint lines running the full width in two directions, shallow recessed pits and channels with clean square edges, long hairline cracks running in a few slow branching lines, and broad sand tongues lying over the slabs with crisp outlines so the joints disappear under them. Every one of these is a large shape. THERE IS NO SMALL DETAIL ANYWHERE: no speckle, no pebble or gravel scatter, no grass tufts, no litter, no stippling and no texture — the surface is made of big clean tonal areas and nothing else.

DENSITY — THIS IS A BACKGROUND, NOT AN ILLUSTRATION, AND IT MUST BE SPARSE: the listed objects are ALL that appear, they are few, and they stand far apart, with AT LEAST HALF THE FRAME carrying nothing but plain quiet surface. THE OPEN GROUND IS EMPTY: no bushes or grass tufts, no loose rocks, no debris, no small marks, no speckle, no busy detail of any kind — bare sand, earth or pavement in large flat shapes only. A crowded or speckled field is a failure. Each object drawn is fully drawn with its real construction — no squiggles or filler; if it cannot be drawn clearly at this size, leave bare ground.

THIS IS THE QUIETEST FIELD OF THE SET AND IT IS MEANT TO BE ALMOST EMPTY: at least three quarters of the frame is nothing but plain open ground. Resist filling it. An empty, well-drawn ground is the correct result; anything added to make it look interesting is a failure, because printed game pieces stand on this surface.

THIS PLACE HAS NEVER BEEN FOUGHT OVER: there is NO crater, NO shell hole, NO burn, NO scorch, NO blackened ground, NO rubble field, NO collapsed building and NO wreck of any kind anywhere in the frame. What makes it look used is AGE, not damage — worn surfaces, faded markings, sand drifted over everything, dust, cracks, empty buildings shut up and left. Nothing here is dark and nothing here is broken open.

MATERIALS READ APART AT A GLANCE: each has its own flat colour meeting its neighbour along a HARD CLEAN EDGE, never a blend. SAND — pale bleached ochre-grey, closer to bone than gold, never vivid yellow; BARE EARTH — duller grey-brown; CONCRETE — pale cool grey in large jointed panels; ASPHALT — medium neutral grey, darker than concrete, never near-black.

NO VEHICLES AND NO MACHINES ANYWHERE: no tank, no truck, no aircraft, no turret, no barrel, nothing that reads as a machine sitting on the ground, and no wreck, no scrap and no torn metal either. The frame holds only ground, surfaces and standing structures.

PLACEMENT: the few objects are spread thinly so no part of the frame is busier than another, with wide open ground between them — two things near each other, then a long gap, then one alone. Never a grid, rows, equal spacing, symmetry or one element per corner. The ground runs off all four edges; objects sit well in from the borders, drawn complete.
