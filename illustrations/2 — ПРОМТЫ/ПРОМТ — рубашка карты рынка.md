# Рубашка карты рынка — 4:3 горизонтально, СТРОГО центрированная перспектива

**Формат.** Широкий горизонтальный кадр 4:3.

**Центрирование (по договорённости).** Точка схода — строго в геометрическом центре кадра, ровно на пересечении горизонтальной и вертикальной середины; проход уходит точно в этот центр, ворота стоят точно на нём. Никаких смещений.

**Как ломается зеркальность.** Только содержанием: левая и правая стороны разные по составу товара, высоте, плотности и ритму, интервалы колонн неравные, фигуры на разной удалённости и в разном количестве. Геометрия перспективы при этом остаётся центральной.

**Светлота.** Задан явный верхний предел темноты: ни одна зона не темнее средне-тёмного серого, стеллажи и товар живут в середине диапазона и остаются читаемыми внутри, тёмное — только в мелких формах (щели, откосы, контур).

Wide horizontal 4:3 composition. Camera at chest height standing in the middle of a wide central aisle, looking straight ahead down its length in strict one-point perspective, so the aisle draws the eye forward through the depth of the hall to an enormous entrance gateway standing open in the far wall.

THE VANISHING POINT SITS EXACTLY AT THE GEOMETRIC CENTRE OF THE FRAME, on the horizontal midline and precisely on the vertical midline; the aisle recedes straight into that exact centre point, the distant gateway is centred on it, and the camera axis points dead ahead with no tilt and no lateral shift. The composition is strictly centred in perspective: the converging lines of the floor, the side rows and the roof structure all meet at that one central point.

THE WIDE FRAME IS USED, NOT PADDED: the empty aisle enters the picture at the bottom as a broad funnel of bare floor spanning most of the lower edge, narrowing steadily and evenly on both sides as it recedes into the centre; the goods and stands fill the entire left third and the entire right third from the bottom edge up to the roof structure, with the nearest large goods on each side cropped by the left and right edges of the frame so the hall clearly continues past the borders; the exposed roof trusses run as a band across the full width of the top of the frame, their lines converging into the same central point. No empty margins anywhere along the sides or the top.

THE CENTRAL AISLE IS COMPLETELY EMPTY ALONG ITS WHOLE LENGTH, from the bottom edge of the frame all the way to the distant gateway: no people, no vehicles, no crates, no goods, nothing standing on it and nothing crossing it — only bare light concrete floor with painted guide lines running away into the depth. Not one figure steps into the aisle anywhere along it. All activity is pressed to the left and right sides and stops dead at the edge of the aisle.

Near-future alternate-history setting: hardened industrial technology and heavy composite military hardware exist, and the material culture reads as precise applied military engineering. Never high fantasy, never a present-day real-world shop or bazaar.

THE PLACE — a colossal covered black-market arms hall inside a light steel hangar, bright and airy, reading as a magnificent illicit showroom rather than a warehouse: soft daylight falls from a clerestory band of windows high under the roof and floods in through the distant open gateway, where the outside light is bright and warm but still holds its shape and detail, never blown out to pure white. Slim steel columns march away down both sides at UNEVEN intervals, pendant lights hang on long stems above the aisle without descending into it, and the floor is light sandy-grey concrete.

VALUE CONTROL: the whole image lives in the upper and middle part of the value range. No area of the picture goes darker than a mid-dark grey — there are no near-black masses anywhere, no black silhouetted shelving, no unreadable dark blocks, no heavy dark walls. The racking, the stored goods and the machines all sit in the MIDDLE of the value range, clearly lighter than their own outlines, so their internal structure stays fully readable: individual shelves, cradles, panel joints, bolted flanges and the separate items stacked in them are all legible rather than lost in shadow. The darkest tones are reserved for small shapes only — shadow gaps, recesses, joints and linework — never for whole objects and never for whole zones of the frame. THE LEFT SIDE AND THE RIGHT SIDE ARE DELIBERATELY DIFFERENT AND NEVER MIRRORED — different content, different heights, different density, different rhythm, a different number of figures — and each side is organised in three clear layers of depth. LEFT SIDE, heavy hardware, lower and more massive. RICHNESS COMES FROM SCALE, DEPTH AND REPETITION, not from small clutter: a few very large objects repeated into the distance, never a scatter of small props.

THE WORLD READS AS CREDIBLE AND SUBSTANTIAL, NOT AS TOYS ON A FLOOR: walls, racks and machines have real thickness, weight and construction — panel joints, bolted flanges, structural ribs, welded seams, scuffed edges and load-bearing frames — and every object looks like it would be heavy to move. PHYSICAL LOGIC: nothing interpenetrates; every object rests on a surface with its base visible and its own contact shadow; nothing floats; nothing is cut by another object without an occlusion edge.

COLOUR SEPARATION: figures, counters, machines, rack walls and capsules each use two to three clearly separated flat hues, all within the mid and upper value range, so every silhouette reads instantly even at small size; the empty aisle stays the palest, cleanest area of the picture so the eye is pulled straight down it into the central gateway.

PALETTE: pale blue-white steelwork and roof, light sandy-grey floor, mid-toned pale grey and soft steel on machines and ordnance, dark olive and slate coveralls on the figures as the only genuinely dark notes and only on small figure-sized shapes, yellow warning striping on the rack uprights as the industrial note, and the amber-yellow glow of the energy capsules as the single strong saturated accent, occupying roughly five to ten percent of the frame. Soft even daylight from the clerestory windows plus warm bright light from the distant central gateway plus the internal glow of the display cases; shadows moderate, crisp and clearly shaped, never black and never absent.

STYLE: cel-shaded comic illustration, clean even outlines, flat colour fills, two-to-three tone hard-edged shading, crisp clean shadow shapes, interior detail conveyed through shadow shapes rather than linework. Coloured linework in darkened, cooler versions of each local colour, never pure black, only slightly heavier on the nearest objects and thinning steadily into the depth — never heavy enough to merge with the fill into a dark mass. Depth conveyed by lowering contrast toward the gateway, never by blur. No readable text, letters, logos or numbers anywhere. Artwork runs off all four edges, no border, no margin, no vignette. Rendered as a clean comic illustration throughout — never photographic, never a realistic rendered texture.
