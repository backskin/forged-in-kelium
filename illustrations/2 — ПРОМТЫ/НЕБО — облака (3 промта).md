# НЕБО С ОБЛАКАМИ — по удачному рендеру (3 варианта)

**Что это.** Промты, написанные прямо под тот рендер, который тебе понравился: плоские облака с чёткой фестончатой кромкой, белое тело плюс одна голубовато-серая ступень с одной стороны, тонкая мягкая тень на небе под каждым, глубокая приглушённая синева фона.

**Важно: здесь я сознательно отменил своё же прошлое правило** про «облако без контура и с плавным спадом плотности». Оно было написано для другого вида облаков; в этом рендере работает ровно противоположное — жёсткая чистая кромка и две плоские заливки. Смешивать эти два подхода нельзя, иначе получится каша.

**Разница между вариантами — только в количестве и раскладке облаков.**

---

## 1. Плотнее, чем на рендере, плюс грозовая масса в углу

20–25 облаков всех размеров и одна большая серая масса в углу.

```
STYLE, NON-NEGOTIABLE: cel-shaded comic illustration — flat colour fills in two clean tonal steps with crisp hard-edged shapes. A drawn illustration, NOT a photograph and NOT a 3D render: no airbrush, no soft blur, no glow, no bloom, no volumetric lighting, no rendered three-dimensional volume, no hatching, stippling, speckle, grain or noise beyond a faint even paper tint. Sharp even focus everywhere. No text, letters or numbers. The image fills the frame and runs off all four edges: no border, no frame, no vignette, no margin.

CAMERA — NADIR, LOOKING STRAIGHT DOWN ONTO THE CLOUD LAYER from far above: everything is flat and parallel, no perspective, no vanishing point, no horizon, no tilt and no straight dividing line anywhere. NO GROUND IS DRAWN — this is the air layer itself; the blue behind the clouds is open air and nothing else.

CONTENTS: a deep blue sky seen from above, well populated with cumulus: TWENTY TO TWENTY-FIVE separate clouds of clearly different sizes spread over the whole tile — FOUR OR FIVE large banks, EIGHT OR NINE middle-sized ones, and the rest small fragments and single lobes; plus ONE much larger grey storm mass in one corner. There is nothing else in the tile at all.

HOW EACH CLOUD IS DRAWN — FOLLOW THIS EXACTLY: every cloud is a FLAT SHAPE with a CLEAN CRISP LOBED OUTLINE, built from rounded bumps of clearly different sizes running along its edge so the silhouette is scalloped and irregular, never a smooth oval and never a symmetrical puff. Each cloud is filled with an off-white body, and along ONE side — the same side on every cloud in the picture — a band of pale blue-grey lies inside the outline as a second flat step, following the lobes and varying in width, reading as the shaded underside. Some clouds also carry a small third patch of that grey deeper inside them. Under each cloud, offset slightly to the same side as the shading, sits a THIN SOFT SHADOW of slightly deeper blue on the sky. Exactly two tones per cloud plus that shadow — no gradient inside a cloud, no rim light, no white glow, no outline drawn in a different colour.

DISTRIBUTION — THIS IS WHAT MATTERS MOST: the clouds are scattered ACROSS THE WHOLE TILE and there is no empty quarter and no obvious pattern. They are of CLEARLY DIFFERENT SIZES, from small fragments no bigger than a thumb to long banks several times that, with every size in between and no two shapes alike. Their spacing is UNEVEN: two or three drift close together and almost touch, one small one sits alone in a wide gap, a few overlap slightly and read as one larger mass with a step of grey where they cross. Several clouds are cut by the edges of the frame and continue off it. NEVER a grid, never a row, never even spacing, never one per corner, never a repeating motif, and never one cloud sitting in the exact middle.

THE SKY: a deep, muted, desaturated navy-blue filling everything between the clouds, faintly mottled with barely visible patches one shade lighter and darker so it is not a dead flat fill, but with NO gradient, NO vignette and NO lighter area suggesting a sun. The blue is calm and slightly dusty, never electric, never turquoise, never a bright cartoon sky-blue.

ONE STORM MASS occupies a corner of the tile — a much larger, denser cloud body of the same flat construction but in cooler mid-greys, its outline just as lobed, its own darker grey step spread wider through it and reaching deeper than on the white clouds. It is the darkest thing in the picture, but it is still a MID GREY: nothing here approaches black. Its ragged margin breaks up into a few small detached fragments that trail away from it into the blue.

COLOUR: off-white cloud bodies, pale blue-grey shading, deep muted navy sky, cool mid-greys in the storm mass. Low saturation, calm and slightly faded, like printed paper. Nothing is pure white, nothing is black, nothing is vivid.
```
